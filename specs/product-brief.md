# Product Brief - BreadboardVision

## Problem
Students beginning their engineering education often struggle with introductory circuit laboratories. A single misplaced jumper wire, reversed LED polarity, or incorrect resistor value on a breadboard can halt progress for hours, leaving students frustrated and waiting for teaching assistant help in overcrowded lab rooms. Existing educational tools are either purely virtual simulation sandboxes or static textbooks that fail to help students debug physical, hands-on hardware components.

## Solution
BreadboardVision is an interactive, AI-powered educational assistant that helps students troubleshoot and debug physical circuits using vision and guided analysis. By processing circuit images (screenshots from web simulators or photos of breadboards) or structured text descriptions, the assistant identifies wiring errors, explains the physical principles broken (e.g., short circuits, incorrect resistance), and provides step-by-step guidance on how to fix them. This creates a self-paced, supportive laboratory experience that accelerates learning and reduces frustration.

## Target Users
- **First-Year Engineering Students:** Beginners learning basic circuits and electronics who lack the experience to troubleshoot their own wiring mistakes.
- **Self-Directed Learners:** Hobbyists and makers building projects at home without access to university TAs or professors.
- **Lab Instructors and TAs:** Educators managing large class sizes who need a tool to handle basic troubleshooting queries so they can focus on complex concepts.

## Success Metrics
- **Instant Error Feedback:** Students receive a structural diagnostic analysis of their circuit within 3 seconds of submission.
- **Self-Sufficiency Improvement:** Over 70% of reported circuit issues are successfully solved by students using the tool's suggestions without needing instructor intervention.
- **High Diagnostics Utility:** Students rate the troubleshooting tips and explanations as helpful or very helpful in 80% of active sessions.

## Unique Value Proposition
Unlike static documentation or automated simulators that only flag compile errors, BreadboardVision bridges the physical-digital divide. It reads circuit states directly from images or structured descriptions and explains the electrical physics behind the error, acting as a virtual tutor rather than a simple syntax checker.
